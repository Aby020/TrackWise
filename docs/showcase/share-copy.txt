Workforce presence platforms break at the endpoints: flaky factory network retries during shift-change, legacy schema collisions, and missed presence checks due to blocked audio assets. We engineered TrackWise to guarantee idempotent state and 32-byte hardened dual-schema resolution—without the unreliability.

Here's how we solved the architectural bottlenecks natively:

↳ Dual-Schema Identity Engine: Secure routing that normalizes auth across legacy `public.users` and `modern.users` in a single transaction. Zero downtime during identity migrations.
↳ Idempotent Shift Engine: Conflict-free concurrent punch resolution. If a spotty network triggers three POST retry spikes, the engine guarantees a single consistent shift state.
↳ Native Web Audio Watchdog: A 30-minute presence ping generated purely via the browser's native AudioContext. No external MP3 assets to block, cache, or fail.
↳ Enterprise Route Shielding: Fail-fast 32-byte JWT guard, cryptographic IDOR defense, and strict ingress rate-limiting before the router even touches the DB.

TrackWise is a high-assurance operations portal built for zero-trust environments.
